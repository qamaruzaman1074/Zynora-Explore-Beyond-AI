# ZYNORA SEO Keyword Map

## Research status

The phrases below are research candidates, not validated keyword targets. No
search-volume, keyword-difficulty, or ranking data was available during this
implementation. Validate them with an external keyword research platform and
Search Console before prioritizing content or making traffic forecasts.

## Keyword themes

| Theme | Research candidate phrases | Search intent | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | AI tools directory; AI tools list; AI tools catalog | Informational / commercial investigation | Core discovery terms; validate wording and demand. |
| Secondary | best AI tools; discover AI tools; AI tools by category; AI tools for productivity; AI tool finder | Informational / commercial investigation | Align with the site's directory and category navigation; validate “best” phrasing before making ranking claims. |
| Long-tail | best AI tools for students; AI tools for business; AI tools for writing and research; AI tools by task | Informational / commercial investigation | Build content only where the actual directory offers relevant listings; avoid implying independent rankings. |
| Pricing-related | free AI tools; AI tools with free plans | Commercial investigation | Treat as research candidates only. Do not promise free access: plans change and catalog pricing may be incomplete. |
| Comparison-related | AI tools comparison; ChatGPT alternatives; alternatives to [tool name] | Commercial investigation | The site is a discovery directory, not an independently tested comparison service. Avoid comparison or endorsement claims without supporting content. |
| Category-specific | AI writing tools; AI image generators; AI video generators; AI coding tools; AI research tools; AI productivity tools | Informational / commercial investigation | Use the corresponding live category pages and actual category descriptions. |
| Individual tool | [tool name] AI tool; [tool name] features; [tool name] official website | Navigational / informational | Tool names and descriptions must come from the catalog; do not imply verified feature or pricing facts that the catalog does not contain. |
